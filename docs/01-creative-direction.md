# flaetchen.com Creative Direction

## Goal
Create a custom, memorable, high-end agency website that demonstrates flaetchen's quality through design, motion and technical execution.

## Brand Foundation
Typeface: Outfit only.

Colors:
- black
- white
- neutral light grey
- grey tonalities
- accent #bcff03

Tone:
- confident
- mature
- precise
- premium
- direct
- visually bold
- technically polished

Avoid:
- gimmicks
- trend stacking
- startup aesthetics
- generic creative-agency language
- visual clutter

## Creative Reference Balance
Preymaker contributes visual power, scale, cinematic project presentation, project focus and restraint.

Ownitt contributes attitude, personality, direct communication, service storytelling and structure.

flaetchen contributes the actual brand: Outfit, black/white/grey, #bcff03, founder presence and German market context.

## Page Rhythm
Use contrast:
quiet → strong → quiet → immersive → quiet → personal → strong finish.

## Homepage Intent
The homepage should establish:
1. who flaetchen is
2. quality of work
3. what flaetchen does
4. how the agency thinks
5. who is behind it
6. where to go next

## Projects
Projects should dominate visually. Use large media, bold crops, video selectively, strong sequencing and case-study transitions.

## Services
Treat services as chapters, not cards.

## Founder
The founder should be part of the brand experience, not a generic biography block.

## Fullscreen Menu
Treat the fullscreen menu as a branded scene.

## Logo
Logo may adapt to dark/light scenes. No decorative logo animation without purpose.

## Creative Test
For every major scene ask:
- could this belong to any other agency?
- does it look templated?
- is there one strong visual idea?
- is the work dominant enough?
- is the typography strong enough?
- is the motion necessary?
